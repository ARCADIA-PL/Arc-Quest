ArcQ's assets/arc_quest/textures/gui/style/moderna.png is adapted from this work.
Changes: extracted transparent metal frame, corner ornament and crystal layers;
cropped and rescaled into an atlas, brightened metalwork, mirrored corner variants,
and converted the crystal to grayscale for dynamic tinting in the owning HUD theme.
No original text, character portrait, spell icon or full-screen artwork is bundled.
The eight-patch frame retains a transparent center for ArcQ's translucent HUDs.
Rebuild script: tools/extract_moderna.py (requires the original PSD, Pillow and psd-tools).
The author does not endorse this adaptation.
