ArcQ modifications: extracted engraved corner ornaments, metal strips, input
borders, button material and frame, circular medallion frame and inventory rim;
cropped and resized them into a 256x256 transparent PNG atlas. Metal is brightened
for Minecraft GUI scales. Button faces and colored frame strips are neutralized
for runtime theme tinting. No original text, inventory icons or opaque panel
interiors are included. Native rendering keeps ArcQ theme/rarity/state colors
and the original scene-panel opacity. Rebuild with tools/extract_wyrmheart.py.

The original PSD and its provenance are stored in the source repository under
art_sources/hud/wyrmheart-rpg-game-ui/ and are not included in the runtime JAR.
